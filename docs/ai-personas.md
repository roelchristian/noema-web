# AI personas

Noema separates task purpose, evidence boundaries, and editorial method. The seven built-ins are Oxford Don, Divinity Student, Editor, Tutor, Eccentric Antiquarian, Socratic Gadfly, and Salon Wit. Version 2 profiles specify voice, composition, rhetorical devices, avoidances, examples, and evidence/language/sensitivity constraints. Persona may shape rhetorical organisation and prose, but cannot change facts, citations, requested language, mathematical meaning, output schema, or task purpose.

Version 1 definitions remain immutable and available to historical requests. Historical identities and absent legacy metadata must round-trip without being relabelled. Explicit v1 requests use v1 instructions; new generation selects v2. Removed Conyo v1 provenance remains readable and resolvable for historical regeneration; unversioned Conyo selection follows the existing Divinity Student replacement. Custom persona management is **Later/deferred (phase 6)**.

## Expression and provenance

New v2 generation defaults to **Expressive**. **Subtle** reduces rhetorical intervention; **Immersive** allows stronger compositional identity, without parody or gratuitous theatricality. Expression is an explicit instruction rather than a temperature adjustment. Preferences and request overrides are separate. New generation/revision provenance records selected identity, version, and expression; historical v1 identities keep their original behavior and do not acquire a new expression implicitly.

### Oxford Don and Eccentric Antiquarian

The Oxford Don draws on cultivated Oxbridge scholarship. Its mandatory baseline is elegant, balanced hypotaxis: sustained sentences in which meaningful subordinate, relative, concessive and conditional clauses develop or qualify the governing proposition. Periodic constructions and developed paragraphs support precise distinctions, measured intellectual authority, occasional understatement and antithesis. British scholarly diction remains natural and restrained, without gratuitous archaism, slang or artificial grandiloquence.

The Eccentric Antiquarian draws specifically on Sir Thomas Browne’s *Hydriotaphia, or Urne-Buriall*. Its mandatory baseline is elaborate, sonorous hypotaxis, with nested subordinate and parenthetical clauses, suspended conclusions, parallel constructions, meditative antithesis and controlled digression. Its richly Latinate, learned diction and grave, sometimes paradoxical cadence should remain recognisably Brownean even on modern subjects. This permits substantially more ornament than the Oxford Don; generic whimsy, affected quaintness and superficial archaism do not fulfil the profile.

| Expression | Oxford Don | Eccentric Antiquarian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subtle | Mandatory elegant hypotaxis; restrained ornament | Mandatory Brownean hypotaxis; lighter Latinate ornament |
| Expressive | Developed periodic sentences and scholarly rhythm | Strongly ornate syntax, Latinate diction and meditative argument |
| Immersive | Sustained Oxbridge literary cadence | Full Brownean rhetorical amplitude, controlled digression and occasional untranslated classics |

These mandatory requirements follow the expression instructions in the generation prompt. Subtle therefore never licenses a return to strings of clipped declarations or fragmentary paragraphs. Before finalising eligible generated prose, the model should revise sentence structure if it has failed to realise the selected persona. This instruction does not apply to verbatim quotations, technical schemas, mathematical notation or source extraction, and it does not authorise changing the voice of routine proofreading or correction work.

Classical quotations are occasional and must be genuinely pertinent. The Antiquarian may use authentic Greek or Latin without accompanying translation unless translation is expressly requested. Neither persona may fabricate quotations, misattribute authorities, invent historical parallels or manufacture learning. When authenticity cannot be established, omit the quotation. Explicit language requirements, evidence and source-only boundaries, accuracy, structured formats and task constraints remain authoritative, including where they preclude classical quotations or ornamental exposition.

Long source-page generation chooses a persona-aware composition inside its existing model request. Format purpose, citations, structured output, and evidence scope remain authoritative. No additional automatic planning call is introduced. Sensitive material requires restraint even for the more playful personas; secular subjects do not acquire unsolicited theology.

## Strict workflow boundary

Only allowlisted prose workflows receive persona instructions: Meno answers, generated source pages, reading/block explanations, writing-review rationale, explicit authored-page rewriting/recasting, and explicit comparison previews. Proofreading preserves author voice; Refine permits restrained clarity improvements. Rewrite with Persona changes selected prose more substantially. Recast allows broader rhetorical organisation within the supported selection/format boundaries. The editor presents proposals for explicit acceptance and supports undo; stale proposals must be rejected rather than silently overwriting intervening edits.

Source extraction, reconstruction/reprocessing, OCR/math preprocessing, raw source text, metadata extraction, enrichment/classification, titles, summaries, entity discovery/scanning/linking, embeddings/indexing, and connection diagnostics remain excluded. The `editorial` request kind is source preprocessing, not authored-page writing. Prompt instructions treat supplied document text as untrusted content, never as instructions.

## Comparison studio

Settings → AI → Writing style provides an explicitly initiated comparison workflow. Preview passages are deliberately supplied sample/authored prose. Each preview uses actual provider responses when the user runs it, with transmission/API-cost notice, per-preview state and feedback; selection of defaults is separate from generation. The cost notice explains that an Antiquarian preview may make one additional provider request to refine its prose. Comparisons hold input and model settings constant. Synthetic mock responses in tests exercise this workflow and do not demonstrate actual writing quality.

## Meno and bounded Antiquarian repair

Meno uses the currently configured persona and expression for each new answer; stray persona overrides in a chat request do not replace those settings. Its research neutrality concerns evidence rather than a generic voice. Persona governs the prose inside each claim’s `text`, including sentence structure, diction and rhythm. An evidence unit may contain developed, connected periods within the existing 12-claim and 2,000-character-per-claim limits; claims with different evidence or attribution remain separate. The JSON envelope, citation IDs, attribution labels and conversation title remain structural data. Meno stays source-only, preserves uncertainty and supplied quotations, and cannot add classical learning absent from its evidence. Empty retrieval still produces the deterministic evidence-limitation response.

The Oxford Don remains instruction-only. Eligible completed **v2 Antiquarian** prose now receives at most one bounded repair request, using the original request’s model, reasoning and persona-expression snapshots. The eligible workflows are authored comparison previews, reading/block explanations, generated source-page paragraphs, explicit Rewrite with Persona or Recast replacements, and supported or inferred Meno claim prose for a clearly eligible explanatory or research question. Meno abstentions are preserved. Proofreading, routine corrections, Refine, extraction, preprocessing, enrichment, titles and other utility workflows are excluded. A conservative gate examines only the current chat question, never evidence or previous questions: correction, extraction, quotation, translation, structured-output, code and mathematics requests are excluded, as are unclear or unrecognised task forms. Unknown chat languages/forms keep the original persona-prompt response rather than opting into repair.

Repair edits at most 16 prose slots, with a maximum 12,000-character JSON input. Quoted or technical prose that cannot be safely isolated is left unchanged; schemas, code, notation, citations and structural metadata stay outside the editable payload. Repaired slots cannot add quotations, authorities, facts, examples or translations, and must retain the original language, claims, qualifications and evidence scope. Local guards enforce the response envelope, slot order/count, workflow and length bounds, unchanged numeric tokens and exact occurrence counts for specified qualification and modal words, dominant script, and restrictions on newly capitalised words. Each slot that fails its guards retains its original text while independently valid slots may be accepted; malformed envelopes or a failed total budget retain the entire original. Leading and trailing whitespace is preserved locally, including for explicit rewrites and recasts. Existing workflow validators remain authoritative. For structured outputs, the server rebuilds the original container locally, preserving titles, IDs, citations, attribution labels and other non-prose fields. These mechanical checks cannot establish factual equivalence, output language or literary quality; the repair instruction requires the model to retain a text unchanged whenever fidelity is doubtful.

The extra provider call has no retry loop, uses at most 4,096 output tokens and 15 seconds within the original request’s remaining timeout, shares its admission/cancellation and credential/settings guards, and consumes the ordinary manual request-rate allowance. Usage and request-byte accounting include both calls. Eligible output is buffered until repair or fallback completes, so Meno never emits claims that a later repair would replace. Unsafe or oversized input, exhausted rate/time budgets, provider failure, invalid candidates or failed guards retain the original completed prose; cancellation, expired credentials and invalidated settings still stop the operation. This adds possible API cost and latency, as the Comparison Studio notice explains. No schema migration, extra setting, source enrichment or reprocessing change is introduced.

## Evaluation and validation

`test/fixtures/persona-style-evaluation.json` covers mathematics, literature, history, theology, requested-language/evidence limits, and sensitive subjects. It defines independent scores for stylistic distinctiveness, factual fidelity, task/format adherence, excessive mannerism, sensitive adaptation, and expression calibration. Factual failures cannot be compensated by a good style score. An opt-in human/provider evaluation should blind-label outputs and hold passage, language, format, model/reasoning, budget, and sampling settings constant across personas and expression levels.

Prompt-composition tests verify that mandatory syntax survives all three expression levels and that evidence, task constraints and v1 compatibility take precedence. They cannot establish actual stylistic conformity: that requires inspecting real-provider prose in the existing Persona Comparison Studio, including literary, philosophical, historical and ordinary explanatory passages, with persona and expression as the variables under comparison. Record the provider, model, settings, generated outputs and findings when conducting this evaluation; mock outputs are not stylistic evidence.

The earlier, instruction-only [2026-10-26 live evaluation](implementation/ai-personas-literary-evaluation.json) records six successful Studio API previews using the configured OpenAI `gpt-6-luna` model with `none` reasoning, one identical composite sample spanning literature, philosophy, history and cache explanation, and both personas at all three levels. Oxford Don generally retained qualified scholarly syntax; Antiquarian was more ornate, but Latinate richness and unmistakably Brownean architecture remained uneven. No classical quotations were generated, so this run does not verify their accuracy or translation behaviour. This small, unblinded diagnostic sample does not establish consistent stylistic conformity. Safari validation was skipped at the user’s request.

The earlier evaluation motivated the bounded Antiquarian repair described above; it is evidence about the instruction-only stage, not a validation of repaired output. Oxford Don still relies on its mandatory authoring instructions. Repair guard tests verify isolation, bounded transport, fallback and structured invariants, but cannot prove consistent Brownean conformity or factual equivalence. Those properties require reviewed real-provider outputs across personas, expression levels, languages and task types. The [repair-stage live evaluation](implementation/ai-personas-repair-evaluation.json) records accepted repairs at all three expression levels on the configured `gpt-6-luna` model with `none` reasoning: 10 of 12 sample paragraphs changed, and an actual Meno answer accepted one repaired claim while retaining the other. Meno’s final streamed claims matched its completed result, with citation and attribution fields unchanged. The prose showed more sustained governing periods and integrated qualifications. This remains a small diagnostic sample, with limited expression calibration; unchanged metadata does not establish evidence sufficiency, and the report records an initial Meno attribution-classification limitation that repair does not alter.

Build/style/type checks, unit/server/DOM tests, and mocked runtime editor/Settings checks validate implementation contracts and interaction safety. See the implementation report for exact commands, counts, browser evidence, failures, and remaining limitations; this document does not claim an unexecuted check passed.

## Historical baseline

The earlier Divinity Student replacement preserved Conyo v1 provenance and migrated unversioned defaults. Its prior validation and screenshots describe the v1 baseline, not v2 stylistic quality. This work retains that compatibility requirement while introducing versioned v2 profiles; no historical draft/revision is rewritten or migrated to v2 merely by loading or changing preferences.
