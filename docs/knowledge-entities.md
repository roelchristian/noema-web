## Mentions, properties, and relationships

- Type `@` to find an entity or create one inline. Entity mentions keep the entity's ID, so renaming an entity updates its displayed name across pages.
- Click or tap an editor mention, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to change its linked identity or remove the link while keeping its wording. Choose **Open entity details** there, or open **Knowledge entities**, to edit its details, properties, and relationships.
- Edit property values directly on the entity homepage or inspector. Definitions select the appropriate control, reference target types and cardinality. Historical literals remain available through an explicit compatibility path; unresolved names do not create canonical entities automatically. The Edit tab retains identity and advanced administration.
- A relationship holds a free-form label and a target (`@Person → sibling → @Person`). Incoming connections appear on the target entity too. The arrow shows the stored direction; reciprocal relationships are not automatically inferred.

## Automatic entity links

After an idle pause (about 1.2 seconds) and before saves, Noema matches note text against existing entities in the current vault and converts whole-name matches into entity references. Matching is local and case insensitive, prefers longer names, and leaves ambiguous duplicate names as text. It also scans table cells while preserving the surrounding formatting. Formatting (including code and link styling) does not affect matching, even when a name spans differently styled segments. Existing references, explicitly excluded text, inline equations, and equation/image blocks are skipped. Both the local matcher and AI entity scanning use these same text eligibility rules. This utility creates no new entities and makes no AI requests; the separate AI entity review remains available.

Add aliases when creating an entity or in its details. Aliases can be shared: if two entities share a name or alias, the matcher uses existing page links as context only when exactly one candidate is already linked. Otherwise the occurrences stay as text for review. Review candidates show their type and existing description. **Link this instance** affects only that occurrence, including after idle matching, saving, and reopening. **Link all** explicitly applies the choice to matching instances on the page. Existing page links retain their previous context behavior.

Highlight text and choose **Link entity** directly from the desktop or mobile selection toolbar. Search by name, alias, or surname; use Up/Down and Enter to pick, or click/tap a candidate. The link preserves your wording and creates no alias by default. Check **Save highlighted wording as a reusable vault alias** only when future matching across the vault should learn that phrase. Vault alias changes are separate from document undo: the result notice links to entity details to manage them. A failed or uncertain alias save leaves the selection unlinked and offers the same management route. Cancelling during an in-flight save prevents later insertion; a completed alias mutation is reported separately.

Changing a link preserves the visible wording and applies only to that occurrence. Removing it preserves the wording as text and excludes that occurrence from automatic retagging. Both actions use normal note undo/redo. Creation from the editor focuses Name, restores the selection or caret on cancellation, and keeps editor focus after completion. Stale insertion ranges are rejected without replacing unrelated text. No existing links or saved aliases are cleaned up retrospectively.

Ambiguity review items are stored in the backend's disposable `page_entity_match_cache` table, keyed by note revision, body, and entity names/aliases. Empty results are cached too. Edits invalidate the note's cache, catalog changes replace stale results on retrieval, and deleted pages lose their cache. Opening a page loads its body and current cache together, so review notices and highlights are present on the first paint. After typing, the editor refreshes these records following the typing buffer and renders the supplied highlight offsets; it does not rescan text to build review items. Resolutions are validated against the cache key and revision, then saved through the normal autosave queue. The cache is safe to rebuild and survives server restarts.

Identification defers while text is selected, a menu or dialog is open, or IME composition is active. Matched references are saved through the existing revision-aware autosave queue; newer edits made during a write remain intact. A small footer beneath the editor shows **Identifying entities…**, **Saving…**, and **Automatically saved**, with **Retry** if saving fails.

## Knowledge entity subtypes

Knowledge entities keep a main type (Person, Place, Event, Creative Work, Organization, Organism, Culture, Source, or Concept) and can have multiple subtypes under that main type, such as a Person who is both an Actor and a Writer. Person defaults include Actor, Writer, Artist, Scientist, Researcher, Philosopher, Historian, Educator, Engineer, Physician, Entrepreneur, Politician, Activist, Journalist, Musician, Composer, Filmmaker, Athlete, and Religious leader. Other built-in subtypes include City, Book, Film, Company, and Website. Concept starts with Mathematics, Science, Social Sciences, Technology, and Philosophy subtypes. AI entity scanning includes inline and display formulas as context while tagging only eligible text. Evidence matching treats spaces, nonbreaking spaces, tabs, and line breaks equivalently and preserves the original surrounding text and formulas. Create custom subtypes under **Knowledge entities → Manage types & subtypes**.

Built-in and legacy main-type fields are available to every subtype; values remain specific to each entity. Subtypes can define additional fields in the type manager. Multiple assigned subtypes inherit the deduplicated union of their fields. Existing entity fields are preserved and imported into their main type’s shared fields. Existing single-subtype selections migrate automatically. Custom subtypes and fields stay within their vault.

## Property evidence and source automation

Property values show manual, imported, AI, derived or legacy-unknown origin where recorded. Existing values are conservatively marked unknown; the system does not invent authorship. Changed source evidence is marked stale and retained. Source author extraction proposes values for explicit review rather than creating entities or rewriting authored credits. Review offers accept, reject, and explicit retain-both for conflicting single-value evidence. Accepting a proposed canonical name establishes manual authority. The review list shows up to 100 proposals in each view.

Imported Source URL and Import Method records retain stable IDs when unchanged. Conflicts preserve authored values and propose changes. Restored source files are paused for background author/enrichment processing until an explicit retry, including legacy backups; restore never starts provider work. Culture:Language entity identity remains separate from extracted/display language metadata.

## Subjects and disciplines

Page titles and inferred Subjects share one background utility request. The fixed disciplinary registry (`src/shared/noema-domains-v1.json`, version 1.0.0) contains 162 domains under 20 roots. Requests include roots and locally relevant descendants rather than the full registry. Subjects use their own content fingerprint, taxonomy version and assignment revision; historical Concept subtype classifications remain readable.

Choose a primary Subject and up to two secondary Subjects in Knowledge, or leave a page unclassified. Manual selections survive content changes and background classification. Manual edits flush pending note saves, check the page revision, and invalidate domain-sensitive matching without requesting a title or embedding. Concept entities have a separate Disciplines field; Subtypes and inherited Properties keep their existing meaning. Registry and manual assignments are available through `GET /api/domains` and `PUT /api/pages/:id/domains` (with `revision`, `primaryDomainId` and `secondaryDomainIds`). Vault backups include assignments and preserve their origins.

Known-entity matching runs locally across all disciplines. Unique single-word Concept aliases still require review; ordinary phrases such as “field of research” are excluded conservatively. A page subject provides context without excluding interdisciplinary mentions. Existing mentions and excluded text remain protected. Explicit AI scans separately propose entities for review, supply lexically relevant existing candidates, validate all domain IDs, and anchor evidence to a specific block, cell and occurrence. Matching and scan caches include domain, taxonomy, alias and policy dependencies. Unchanged scans reuse saved results; edits made during generation or before acceptance reject stale outputs.
